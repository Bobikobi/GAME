# Trademark policy

The code of this game is free software and its content is free content, and anyone may fork them. The game's **name** and **logo** are not part of that: they tell players which game is the real one, and this page says how you may use them. It follows the Model Trademark Guidelines and the policies of projects such as Godot and Mozilla, in short.

**The Name.** In this policy, "the Name" is the game's name as the home page shows it. It is written here once, and only here:

> `Loomvale` (a working name; it may change, and when it does this line is the one line to change)

**The Logo** is the game's logo as published on the home page.

The owner of the project claims the Name and the Logo as trademarks. Nothing is registered yet; a filing waits for the name to be settled. This page applies to the Name and the Logo whether or not they are registered.

## You may, without asking

- Say your work is **made for** the Name: "made for the Name" on an extension, a pack of art, a recipe list, a tool or an add-on, and "for the Name" or "compatible with the Name" in its title or description.
- Run a fan site, a wiki, a Discord or a forum about the game, if its name says what it is ("an unofficial fan site for the Name") and does not look like the game's own.
- Make and share videos, streams, guides, articles, screenshots and reviews that show the game and use the Name to say which game it is.
- Say your fork is **based on** the game, and link to it, in plain text.
- Use the creator signatures and extension names that you chose; they are yours.

## You may not, without written permission

- Call a different game, or a fork of this one, by the Name, by a name that is easy to mistake for it, or with the Logo.
- Run a server, an app, a store listing, a domain, a channel or an account whose name or look says it is the official game or the official community.
- Use the Name or the Logo on things you sell, or in a way that says the project made, checked or endorsed something it did not.
- Put the Name or the Logo in the name of a company, a product or a token.

A fork that follows the licences and picks its own name is welcome, and the first line of its page may say "based on the Name".

## How to say it

- Use the Name as plain text, spelled as above, and do not alter the Logo.
- Say "unofficial" or "made for the Name" when the project did not make the thing.
- If it could be read as official, add "not affiliated with the Name" where a reader will see it.

## The licences do not cover this

The AGPL-3.0 of the code and the CC BY-SA 4.0 of the content give you rights in the code and the content. Neither gives a right to the Name or the Logo, and the contributor agreement ([CLA.md](CLA.md)) does not either.

## Asking

If a use is not clearly in the first list, ask before you start: open an issue on this repository with the question and what you plan to make. A use that is clearly in the first list needs no answer, and one that breaks the second list is asked to stop, politely, before anything else.
