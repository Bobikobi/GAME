# The art density on a 2K phone, three stills and two videos (2026-10-18)

Made after the owner rejected the 32x48 faces and set the target device: "minimum 2K", "the 2024 flagship models and up". Each frame is 1440x3120, the screen of a 2024 flagship, with a tile of 128 device pixels (about 11 tiles across). Nothing is built: no game code changed.

One difference: the grid Pell is drawn on. The meadow under him is the same tile in all three and is not under test.

| Frame | Pell | One art pixel | Tests |
|---|---|---|---|
| `density-32` | 32x48 | 4 device pixels | The 32 px standard of the morning: the face has no room. |
| `density-64` | 64x96 | 2 device pixels | Room for eyes with whites, brows, a nose, a moustache, the basket's weave. |
| `density-128` | 128x192 | 1 device pixel | The most a 1440 px screen can show. |

Pell was redrawn by Codex's image generation (`pnpm kit:gen`, on the subscription) from the stock `chars/trader` sprite, with the face spelled out in the prompt (about 20 px wide at 64), and imported onto the grid in the core palette. One image each.

Then two 5 s videos of the same walk across a meadow with oaks, at 1440x3120, deleted after the pick: at 64 (Pell 64x96, the grass 64x64, an oak 128x128) and at 128 (Pell 128x192, the grass 128x128, an oak 256x256).

## Question

How much detail can a 2K phone show before the extra pixels stop being seen?

Picked: 64 (DECISIONS 2026-10-18 "The art moves to 64 px per tile"). The owner on the stills: "B came out excellent". On the videos: "no difference on a phone, so 64 for now". Two faults of the videos are rules now: the oak stood barely taller than Pell (STYLE.md section 1b, proportions), and the grass was poorer than the live game's ground (STYLE.md section 3).
