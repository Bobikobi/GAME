# The nature sheets: scale, climate, season, weather and shade on the ladder's square (2026-10-21)

The direction step of [NATURE.md](../NATURE.md) section 8. The scene is the 2026-10-20 ladder's square ([2026-10-20-art-ladder.md](2026-10-20-art-ladder.md)) in its L1 material look, composed by `tools/stills/nature.py` from the tables in `tools/stills/nature.json`. One difference per frame. Nothing in the game changed: no kernel, content, schema or sprite under `content/` was touched. Every frame is 390x844 art pixels at 64 px per tile, enlarged 3x to 1170x2532.

Every prop but Pell is the ladder's script-drawn stand-in. The house's windows are lit at every hour because the stand-in draws them so; the sheets judge the world's laws, not the house.
