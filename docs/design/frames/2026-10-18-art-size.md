# The art size, three stills (2026-10-18)

Stills for the owner's pick: how big one art pixel should be. All three frames are 1170x2532, a 390x844 phone at 3x, showing twelve tiles across as the game shows today (a tile is 96 device pixels). Nothing is built: no game code changed.

One difference: the size of the grid each sprite is drawn on. The scene is the same in all three (grass, two oaks, the elder, a lantern).

| Frame | Grid | One art pixel | Tests |
|---|---|---|---|
| `art-size-16` | tiles and items 16x16, characters 16x24, the oak 32x32 | 6 device pixels | Today's kit, from `content/kit/core`. |
| `art-size-32` | tiles and items 32x32, characters 32x48, the oak 64x64 | 3 device pixels | Twice the detail, still clearly pixel art. |
| `art-size-48` | tiles and items 48x48, characters 48x72, the oak 96x96 | 2 device pixels | The most detail before the pixels stop showing. |

`art-size-detail` crops the elder and the lantern out of each frame at 1:1 device pixels, 16, 32 and 48 from left to right.

## How the 32 and 48 sprites were made

Each was redrawn by Codex's image generation (`codex exec`, on the subscription) with the stock 16 px sprite as the reference image, on a flat magenta background, then imported by `tools/src/kit/import.ts` (grid detection, palette snap to the 32-colour core palette, hard alpha). A white background keys out the elder's white beard; magenta does not. The grass, which fills its tile edge to edge, was cropped and snapped by hand because the importer reads a full-bleed border as background, and it is tiled mirrored in the frame.

Measured: 60 to 116 s and 11k to 31k Codex tokens per image. The model does not keep an exact grid: the first elders came back on a coarser grid than asked, and spelling out the detail (the face, the buttons, the laces) brought the count up.

## Question

Which size keeps the top-down pixel look and gives the most detail a phone can show? Not a move to hi-res 2D: the pixels must stay visible.

Picked: 32 (DECISIONS 2026-10-18). Superseded the same day by 64, for 2K phones: [2026-10-18-density.md](2026-10-18-density.md).
