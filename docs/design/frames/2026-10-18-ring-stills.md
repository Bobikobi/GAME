# The first ring's three stills (2026-10-18)

Six stand-in stills at phone size (390 x 844 art canvas on a 32 px grid, enlarged 3x), made with Pillow from the core 32-colour palette; no mist, night or HUD. Each pair differs in one thing. They test the direction, not the final look. The generator was `make.py`, kept only in the session's `.scratch/` and deleted with it.

| Pair | A | B | Picked |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 scale of the grounds | `ring-s1-a-sweeps`: big sweeps, one ground at about 14 tiles | `ring-s1-b-patchwork`: a patchwork at about 3 tiles | A |
| S2 the valley's frame | `ring-s2-a-flat`: flat ground, a river | `ring-s2-b-levels`: levels with rock faces and steps, a river, a valley framed by mountains and a pass | B |
| S3 how many props | `ring-s3-a-sparse`: sparse, the boathouse's smoke on the skyline | `ring-s3-b-dense`: dense, the same smoke | B |

The owner, 18.10: "it doesn't matter to me, everything looks good, except the straight path, which is too boring". He left the pairs to the manager, who picked A, B, B on what open-world games do. The path rule (GAME_RULES G69) follows from his words.
