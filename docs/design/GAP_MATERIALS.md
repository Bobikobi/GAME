# Loomvale's materials against nine crafting games: what a player eats, wears, heals with, builds and trades (2026-11-02)

Written 2026-11-02 by an outside developer for the owner. Nothing here is built. The page measures the 158 materials of [MATERIALS_COUNT.md](MATERIALS_COUNT.md) and the chains of [PRODUCTION_MAP.md](PRODUCTION_MAP.md) against nine games that live on what a player makes, says where we are thin and where we are already better, and lays an improvement plan in build order. Every step of the plan is a rule or a content row for a domain page; this page decides nothing by itself.

**What it does not change.** No rule, no param, no check row and no content. The three pages of this pull request (this one, the count and the production map) are documentation: a mirror held up to `develop` at ebd425b2c and to the open gear pull request (#609, the pages CLOTHING, WEAPONS, QUARRYING and BUILD_SET, cited as "PAGE section (#609)"). The pull request that carries these pages is based on develop 7cd5aa852; between the two commits DECISIONS.md gained twenty lines at its end and no other file the three pages cite changed, so every anchor here still holds. The collisions of section 7 are read against the rules as they stand; where the plan is better than an old rule the table says how the rule improves, and the pull request that builds the step is the one that amends the rule, never this page.

**The one number.** Wherever a total is named it is the count's: **158 materials = 17 built + 127 planned in landed pages + 14 planned only in #609**, raw 97 and processed 61 (MATERIALS_COUNT.md, Totals). No other total appears on this page.

## 1. The nine games in six paragraphs, and ours

**Old School RuneScape** (Jagex). Twenty-four skills; the materials are long flat ladders, one name per rung: 17 ores and 13 bars ([S1](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Category:Ores), [S2](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Smithing)), 18 logs, 15 herbs, 7 hides and 8 leathers ([S3](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Leather)), about 40 fish; 73 cooked foods ranked by the health one inventory slot gives back ([S4](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Food)); 10 status effects, 4 of them with a cure (poison, disease, bleed, corruption), and about 70 potions ([S5](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Herblore), [S6](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Status_effects)); 24 house rooms and 522 furniture pieces ([S7](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Construction)); 20 light sources ([S8](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Light_source)); 12 dyes ([S9](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Dye)); a market with a 2% tax and a spell that sets a floor price on every item ([S10](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Grand_Exchange), [S11](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/High_Level_Alchemy)). Chains are two to four steps and every intermediate is tradeable. Every piece of gear above the first rung has a level gate; bronze, iron and plain leather are free.

## 2. How to read this page

- **A material** is an ingredient of making, by the count's rule (MATERIALS_COUNT.md, Totals): a raw find or a processed intermediate. Dishes, garments and tools are what the materials become and are counted here as products, not as materials.
- **Built** means it runs on `develop` at ebd425b2c, code and content. **Landed** means a page on `develop` describes it and nothing runs it. **#609** means only the open pull request's pages describe it. **Hole** means a material or step that no page gives a source, station or time for (PRODUCTION_MAP.md section 5).
- **The reference range** is the smallest and largest figure among the nine games on that row, with the game named. A range is a measure of what players of this kind of game have seen, not a target: the verdict says whether we should sit inside it, below it on purpose, or above it.
- **A verdict word.** *Thin*: below the range with no decision saying so. *Thin by decision*: below the range because a rule or the owner says so, and the rule is named. *In range*: inside it on paper, with the built share given. *Ahead*: a structure no reference has.
- Anchors are `path:line` on `develop` at ebd425b2c, "PAGE section (#609)" for the open pages, and `[S-n]` style links for the other games; every link is listed under Sources.

## 4. The critical gaps, by urgency

Urgency words: **now** (the gear wave's own pull requests cannot be read without it), **with #609** (the first follow-up after the open pull request lands), **next docs pass**, **decision** (the owner's word, no build), **with the next land**.

1. **The take verb and the snare: 119 of 158 materials wait behind a verb that does not exist. Now.** Every animal material (hide 61, bone 36, feather 11, sinew 10, organ 1) starts with "take" at the animal's spot: 107 of 374 steps, 0 built (PRODUCTION_MAP.md section 4, stations table). The kernel says so itself: "Nothing here takes an animal: a carcass is laid by the hunt and by tests until then" (kernel/src/hunt.ts:2-5). The dress is built (kernel/src/step.ts:119) and 17 of its 107 steps run, so the chain is cut at its first link only. Inside the take, one tool carries 62 of the 107: the snare, which on `develop` is a line of a table (2 reed cord, 1 branch, work stone; docs/design/HUNT_LADDER.md:15) and has its recipe only in WEAPONS 5.4 (#609). The three built meats cannot be reached in play either (content/core/items/goods.json:43, :79, :148), so the food row above is 9 reachable items, not 12. No reference has this shape. In Valheim, Minecraft, Terraria, Green Hell and Don't Starve the first animal falls in the first minutes of play (Valheim's deer and boar, [V1](https://valheim.fandom.com/wiki/Materials)); Red Dead scripts it as the first chapter's hunt ([R8](https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/Hunting)); Albion's first animal is a tier-1 skinning node ([A2](https://wiki.albiononline.com/wiki/Resource)); Stardew has no hunt. ## 5. Where we are already better

Said once, so the thin rows above are read in proportion.

- **Hide has stages.** Raw hide, scraped hide, then leather or fur by one choice, then boiled, oiled or waxed (docs/design/MATERIALS.md:85-89). Valheim and Red Dead use the hide raw; RuneScape tans in one paid step ([V1](https://valheim.fandom.com/wiki/Materials), [R3](https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/Trapper), [S3](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Leather)). The processing chain is where our count's 61 processed materials come from, and it is the one place the atlas is deeper than every reference.
- **Templates over a class.** The dyed cord is one template over `class: plant, family: berry, min_tier 1` (content/core/templates/dye-cord.json:27-29); a new berry joins without a new recipe (G45, docs/design/GAME_RULES.md:515). Every reference writes one recipe per item.
- **The ladder is a check, not a habit.** One tier up at most per recipe, a level band per tier, a station of the tier, a profession for every recipe (tools/src/check.ts:409-421; G41 docs/design/GAME_RULES.md:488, :740). The references balance their ladders by hand and by patch.
- **No level wall on wearing** (DECISIONS.md:386, :1373): a made thing can be worn by whoever holds it; RuneScape gates every piece above the first rung by a combat level, only bronze, iron and plain leather are free ([S14](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Equipment)).
- **A miss keeps half** (G40; content/core/params.json:122). RuneScape's iron smelt loses the ore half the time ([S2](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Smithing)).
- **Rarity is a world budget** (G42, docs/design/GAME_RULES.md:494; content/core/params.json:82; the check at tools/src/check.ts:472; the table row GAME_RULES.md:741). No reference bounds its supply of rares as a sum; they tune drop rates one by one.
- **Health is one bar and nothing empties by the clock** (G32; docs/design/GAME_RULES.md:352, :625). Valheim learned this and Don't Starve did not ([V5](https://valheim.fandom.com/wiki/Food), [T2](https://dontstarve.wiki.gg/wiki/Crock_Pot/DST)); we have it as a rule with a forbidden pattern.
- **No gear answers a hazard, and power is never sold** (G34, G39; docs/design/GAME_RULES.md:387, :471). Neither has a counterpart in any of the nine; Albion sells premium and RuneScape sells bonds ([A5](https://wiki.albiononline.com/wiki/Premium)).
- **The hide carries the coat** (DECISIONS.md:3876): a garment's colour is the animal's, so looks come from the hunt and not from a dye list.
- **Integers and a server seed** (R2, R4, docs/kernel-review-v1.md:91, :97; G102, docs/design/GAME_RULES.md:303) and a shop invariant that is a theorem (R8, docs/kernel-review-v1.md:109): the economy's floor is proven, not patched.
