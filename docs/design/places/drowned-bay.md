# Place card: the drowned bay (proposal, for the manager and the owner)

Form: PLACE_LOOK section 1. Words only; no picture is an input here. Nothing is built from this before the owner sees one still of it.

| Field | Card |
|---|---|
| World and people | The neutral valley's edge and the way to the human world across the bay (DECISIONS 2026-10-22, decision 24). Ferry people, fishers, the mender of the causeways. |
| Climate | Temperate, wet (NATURE section 2). |
| Materials | Pale dressed stone, wet stone, moss, reed, old timber piles. At most five. |
| Shapes | Roofless towers and arches standing in shallows; low causeways of stepping stones; islets with a few trees; every upright thing taller than the frame at the near end. |
| Ramps | water, stone, grey, moss, teal, and green for the islet trees (six). Blue flowers only in the foreground bank, in the teal ramp. |
| Accent | Teal of the open water. The only warm spot is the player's red. |
| Light and air | Soft overcast to bright morning; thin fog banks lying between islets (the engine's fog layer); cast shade of a tall ruin on the water. A reflection layer is not built on develop yet; the card asks for it, it does not assume it. |
| Landmark | One tall pale tower that runs out of the top edge of the frame near the walking line, and stays readable as the ground's far, tallest shape. No sky, no horizon, no far castle in the frame: the far shore is the next screen's ground. |
| What is there | Causeway stones (to draw), standing arch (to draw), half-sunk tower base (to draw), reed (exists), islet tree (exists), a ferry post (to draw). |
| Never here | Sand beach, palms, red brick, a lit modern window, straight canal walls, anything that is not wet or old. |

## Story lines (three, for STORY.md S1 to S6)

Each line is a person's, never the narrator's; no line proves one reading (S4).

1. Ferryman: "My gran said the towers were taller once. I only know they are wet."
2. Fisher: "I heard a bell under the water. I did not stay to hear it twice."
3. Causeway mender: "Step on the grey stones. The green ones I would not trust."
