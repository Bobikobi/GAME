# Quarrying: stone and what follows (2026-10-31)

Status: Proposed 31.10 by an outside developer. Nothing here is built except the jackpot's experience (section 9; #592, merged 01.11); the build list in the appendix is what the wave's pull requests build, each on the owner's merge.

**What it does not change.** Stone stays mined as decided: a mined node is the kernel's `gather` with `work: "mine"`, it runs by itself and trains Digging (DECISIONS 2026-10-25 "Stone is mined: blows that run by themselves, as felling, training digging"; kernel/src/state.ts:518-522). The river stones, the keystone, the five stone recipes and the camp parts keep their numbers, so no value in content/values.lock.json moves (R11). Metal waits for its discovery (G45; DECISIONS 2026-10-09 "Metal: the grammar is ready, the content waits for its discovery"), the world stays at `open_tiers` 3 (content/core/params.json:88), and the forge and the furnace are the city's (DECISIONS 2026-10-22 "Making in the neutral valley (decision 51 of 22.10)"). Quarrying never changes a tile's level or opens a crossing (G68). A craft miss keeps only inputs (G40); a gather miss stays the CR-60 table's (G15). Tools keep working from the bag (`bestTool`, kernel/src/step.ts:1736-1746); only its tie rule changes (section 6). Each player keeps their own yield (DECISIONS 2026-10-09 "In a shared world each player gathers their own yield, except in the areas between players"). EQUIPMENT 3.4 (mending), PROGRESSION 4.2 (the school catalogue) and 5.1 to 5.7 (gear) are built on, never restated in a second form. Where WEAPONS.md already designed a thing that also touches stone (the `mend` mark, WEAPONS 7), this page takes its design as written.

### 2.2 The required pick, priced

On the seam, `tool: "pick"` makes the pick required (kernel/src/step.ts:620, "You need a pick for this.", :1748-1752) and switches the helper off (:622): bare hands and the hammer are refused there. A required tool gives `150 * (tier - 1)` (:626), so a tier-1 pick adds nothing, a tier-2 pick 150 and a tier-3 pick 300, where the same picks add 150, 300 and 450 as helpers on the river stones.

The proposal keeps :626 as it is and prices the step instead: **the seam's 6000 ms is its time with a tier-1 pick**, the slowest worker the rules allow, which is also what the solver assumes (it prices the base `active_ms`, values.ts:81-97). This is the axe's and the trowel's rule already: the tier-1 tool is the baseline, and each tier above it is a step faster. The alternative (owner question 2, C) changes step.ts:626 so that on a mined node a pick always gives 150 a tier; then the seam's price would assume a bare-hands time nobody can play, and the solver would over-price greenstone by a step.

Two built facts make the requirement fair:
- The tier-1 pick is a start-known recipe at level 2 (section 6), and the skeleton starts with one (the rust pick, outfits.json:125-147). The decision's "bare hands mine, so no race and no opening is locked out" holds for every node below tier 3; a new check row (section 16) refuses any required tool kind that no start-known recipe or shop puts in every race's reach.
- A pick node must also say `work: "mine"`: `nodeVerb` reads the tool first and `work` second, so a `tool: "pick"` node without `work: "mine"` would train Foraging (kernel/src/state.ts:518-522). The client runs it either way (`BY_TOOL.pick` is `mine`, game/src/work.ts:13). A check row closes this (section 16).

The power cap: to stand at the seam a player has Digging 6, so the character level is at least 6 and the cap at least 1600 (content/core/params.json:66; kernel/src/state.ts:525, :536-540). A tier-3 pick (300) with both stone nodes of the school (50 and 80) sums 430, under the cap: nothing is clamped. At character level 1 the cap is 1000 and no tool helps at all (`powerCap`, kernel/src/state.ts:536-540); a new player's hammerstone gives +100 at character level 2 (cap 1100) and its full +150 from character level 3 (cap 1200).

### 2.3 Hours and seasons

**Hours (built, G42-conform).** The seam is worked from 6 to 18 (proposed): by day the sun on the cut thaws it; at night the cold highland locks it. `hours` is a built gate (schema/src/index.ts:346-348; kernel/src/step.ts:615-617), used by four nodes today (content/core/resources/bell-bushes.json, forest-mushrooms.json, moon-reeds.json, sun-herbs.json), counted by V-2 (tools/src/check.ts:214-218), and named by G42 among the gates ("their hours"). A day is 1200000 ms (params.json day_length_ms), so the seam is open half of each 20-minute day and never more than ten minutes away.

**Seasons (wait).** The kernel reads no season: `SEASONS` exists only for a creature group's future spawner (schema/src/index.ts:1860-1861), and NATURE.md:87 says "A season never gates content". When seasons are read, a seasonal quarry node follows G42's written exception: its output shares its class with a thing found all year, so no recipe, step or tool waits for the season (a frost-split ledge in winter that gives more slate, never a stone found only then).

## 4. Gems and side finds

### 4.1 Dig spots: built

A dig spot is a find table today: up to 8 finds with `chance_pct` and `share_pct`, a level, an optional tool, a count and a respawn (schema/src/index.ts:371-399); one keyed roll walks the table (kernel/src/step.ts:666, :671-679), luck tilts only rare and legendary rows (:665, :673), and the solver prices it as `dig` (kernel/src/values.ts:98-117). A searchable is a second built table, drawn once a day (schema/src/index.ts:410-425). The gem pocket needs no code:

- `core/glint-pocket` (proposed): hint "Something catches the light in the cut."; trowel required; level 6; `active_ms` 7000; `respawn_ms` 300000; count 2; finds rock crystal 25 (share 20), agate 8 (share 40), garnet 3 (share 40). Chances sum to 36, under the 100 the schema allows (:374). Computed ranks: rock crystal uncommon, agate rare, garnet rare. Effort is dig at tier 3, 1000 permille (values.ts:30-33).

Prospecting for it is the built hint: the spot carries a `hint`, and Digging's Old signs (level 2, `second_hint`) widens how far a hint is sensed (kernel/src/reactions.ts:144; content/core/professions/digging.json).

### 4.2 Side finds on a gathered node: unbuilt

A resource has one output (`output`, `yield`; schema/src/index.ts:293-369), the kernel takes only that (kernel/src/step.ts:633), and the solver prices one output at share 100 (kernel/src/values.ts:94). The nodes that want a side find:

| Node | Side find | Why |
|---|---|---|
| scree fan | flint | the first flint is picked from loose stone |
| slate ledge | rock crystal | crystal grows in the ledge's cracks |
| greenstone seam | garnet | the seam's rare stone |
| later: the chalk band, the ore veins | flint; a vein's second mineral | as the land holds them |

**The kernel shape (proposed):**

1. **Schema:** `ResourceBody.finds?: Find[]`, 1 to 4, the dig spot's own row shape (schema/src/index.ts:371-378). The main output's share is 100 minus the finds' shares, at least 1; chances sum to at most 100.
2. **One pure function**, `pickFind(finds, roll, gradePct, luckPermille, rankOf)` in `kernel/src/finds.ts`: the loop of kernel/src/step.ts:671-679 moved out whole (the chance times `GRADE_PCT[grade]`, the luck tilt on ranks 2 and up, the cumulative edge). `dig` calls it, unchanged in behaviour.
3. **`gather` reads it** after a blow that yields (grades 1 to 4): one keyed roll `drawBasisPoints(seed, nodeId, draws)` and `draws + 1`, the stream the grade-0 keep already uses (:630-631), so the server's seed draws every find (G102) and a replay gives the same find. On grade 4 at a node that plays a minigame (section 10's predicate) and has finds, the table is drawn once more: the next draw of the same stream, so the server draws it too and a replay repeats it (section 9). The main output never grows. A find that fits goes to the bag. A find that does not fit is laid on the player's ground pile at their tile, the same pile `drop` keeps (step.ts:1769-1782; DECISIONS 2026-10-23 "The owner's answers on the bag: stacks, the belt, putting things down"), with an event that says it lies there. The node's main output still needs room, as built (:623-624).
4. **Kept apart from the miss table.** Side finds roll on a success; G15's miss table (CR-60) rolls on grade 0 and stays its own table. Mined nodes run at grade 3 (game/src/scenes/world.ts:3344; the kernel's rule after section 10) and never miss.
5. **The solver** (kernel/src/values.ts:81-97): a resource's outputs become the main item at its share and each find at `num: chance_pct * rolls`, `den: 100`, `share: share_pct`; the rarity bonus takes the rarest of output and finds, as a dig spot's does (:105-107). `rolls` is `jackpot_find_rolls`, {2, 1..3, 1} (proposed), on a node that plays a minigame (the shared predicate of section 10, never the bare presence of `game`), and 1 on every other node. V-2 ranks a find by the same rolls (point 6), and the rarity bonus follows that rank: it reads the declared rarity (:70-74), which V-2 holds within one rank of the computed one. So D2 holds (a client that claims grade 4 on every blow earns what the solver already counts) and G102 holds (the claim names a grade; the server's seed draws the find).
6. **The check:** computedRarity ranks a node's find by its chance times its rolls (point 5), as it ranks a dig find by its chance (tools/src/check.ts:207-213), so V-2 sees flint, crystal and garnet; and a side find is never above its node's output tier, so a tier-1 node never hands out a tier-3 gem (G41).

The rare-find chime is G25's ("The rare find is heard"); a side find of rank rare or better plays it.

## 5. Reading the stone: prospecting is a sight

A sight is "information, not power" (PROGRESSION 4.4, :218): it changes what the client may show, never what a rule answers.

- **Now, with no code:** each stone gets its own look (drawn sprites, Waits; until then kit stand-ins, the appendix, and the ledge and the seam share the keystone's frame), so the tier reads from across the screen once drawn; the empty sprite shows a worked node (`sprite_empty`); the node's description names what it may hold in words ("Flint turns up in it."); the glint pocket is found by its hint and Old signs (4.1).
- **With side finds:** when a player looks at a node, the client lists its side finds by rank word (often, now and then, rarely), never a chance or a percentage (DECISIONS 2026-10-22 "No percentages on screen (decision 39 of 22.10)"). The table is content the client already holds.
- **Later, Mining's `vein_sense`** (PROGRESSION.md:154, :218): ready nodes in view that can hold a rare-or-better find show a faint vein. It never shows the next draw: knowing which node's next blow finds would let a player choose blows, which is power, not sight. Smithing's `alloy_read` needs it (PROGRESSION.md:540).

## 10. Grade authority for automatic work, and the one minigame predicate

**The finding.** Automatic work has no minigame, and the client sends grade 3 for every mined, felled or dug action (game/src/scenes/world.ts:3344). The server's API accepts any integer grade from 0 to 4 for `gather` and `dig` (`isAction`, server/src/api.ts:158-159, :172-185), and the kernel checks only the range (`checkGrade`, kernel/src/step.ts:1688). Until #592 a modified client that claimed 4 on every automatic blow earned three times the experience. Since section 9's first part landed (#592, merged 01.11) the gain is gone (experience x1, step.ts:640, :686), but the claim still writes a "jackpot" event for work that had no strip (:641, :687), and the false event stays until the kernel reads the grade as below.

**Which nodes play a minigame is the client's rule, and `game` is not it.** The client starts automatic work whenever `resourceKind` returns a kind (game/src/ui/ui.ts:644), before it reads `game` (:645): a resource is automatic when its tool is an axe, a trowel or a pick, or when it names no tool and says `work: "mine"` (`BY_TOOL`, `resourceKind`, game/src/work.ts:13-22). The oaks and the fallen logs carry `game: "snap"` with `tool: "axe"` (content/core/resources/oak-trees.json, fallen-logs.json) and are felled by themselves at grade 3. A dig spot is automatic when its tool is one of those three (`treasureKind`, game/src/work.ts:25-27) and is taken by hand at grade 3 when it names no tool (game/src/ui/ui.ts:665-673); only a dig spot with another tool plays the timing game, and none does today. A node with neither `game` nor an automatic kind plays "timing" (ui.ts:645). Keyed on `game`, the rules of sections 4.2 and 9 would let a modified client claim grade 4 on every tree and would price a tree's finds at two jackpot rolls that honest play never reaches.

**The proposal (in the build list).** One pure predicate in the kernel: `autoKind`, `resourceKind` and `treasureKind` move from game/src/work.ts into the kernel, and the client imports them, so both sides read one rule. A node or spot plays a minigame exactly when that predicate gives no kind and, for a dig spot, it names a tool. On a node or spot that does not play one, the kernel takes the grade as 3 whatever the action says, before anything is computed. The rule lives in the kernel because the server runs the kernel's step on every action; the API stays a shape check (`isAction`, server/src/api.ts:158-159: "the kernel's shape, and no field besides it"). Minigame nodes keep the client's grade and D2's worst-case pricing (section 9), and sections 4.2 and 9 and the solver read the same predicate. Existing tests that send grade 0 to the river stones to prove the miss draw (kernel/test/grades.test.ts:36-60; invariants.test.ts:92-124) move to a minigame node.

**Against G102 and the refusal list.** G102 refuses "a client that sends a change the kernel did not make" (docs/design/GAME_RULES.md:303-308), and stream 18 a)'s refusal list drops a request that carries gold, the bag, a position beyond one legal step, or a tick or time of its own (docs/team/NEXT.md:516). A grade is none of these: it is a parameter of a legal action, as the node's id is, not state, and the kernel makes every change that follows from it. So a claimed 4 on automatic work is not refused: the action is legal, the kernel ignores the claim and reads 3, and the reply is grade 3's, with no `jackpot` event. Every draw stays on the server's seed, which never reaches a client (R4, docs/kernel-review-v1.md:97), so no claim picks a find.

9. **Portals and ore.** Whether a shortcut near a mine re-prices anything is open with D5 (docs/design/OPEN_WORLD.md:219-221, :475-481). Either way a fast route carries no ore: it "moves the person and not the basket" (:219).
