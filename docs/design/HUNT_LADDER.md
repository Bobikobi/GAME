## 6. The pay cap in value units (HUNTING 9)

HUNTING 9 caps a hunt at combat.pay_per_s_mg (50 mg) for each second of its loop. A shop pays 5 mg for each value-ms, so one second at w = 600 is 3000 mg: the param as written is about 60 times too small to be a cap on payFor (the hare: cap 2450 mg, payFor 147000 mg). The proposed check is in value units: value per active second, the rarity bonus included, never above effort_permille[hard] = 1000. Without the bonus the rate is the tier's w, at most the hard tier's by construction, so only a rarity bonus can pass the cap; whether a bonus counts toward it is the owner's call.
