## 1. The two games in one paragraph each

**Old School RuneScape** keeps 24 skills: 7 for combat, 5 for gathering, 6 for production and 6 for utility ([S1](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Skills)). Its gear runs through separate skills: Crafting spins, weaves, sews leather and hide armour and sets jewellery ([S2](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Crafting)); Mining breaks rock ([S8](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Mining)); Smithing smelts bars and forges metal ([S10](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Smithing)); Fletching makes bows and arrows ([S12](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Fletching)). Every rung is a level to make, and the better worn rungs ask a level to wear ([S4](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Studded_body), [S9](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Pickaxe)). Degradable items lose charges, and at zero they crumble, revert, break to be repaired for a cost, or keep working with less ([S6](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Item_degradation)), and a central exchange takes a tax on each sale and removes most of it from the game ([S18](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Grand_Exchange)).

**Loomvale** has 6 professions (content/core/professions/: cooking, digging, fishing, foraging, handcraft, woodcutting). One craft profession, Handcraft, makes worn pieces and tools at the workshop, whose tier is the recipe's (kernel/src/step.ts:201-206). Stone is mined and trains Digging (DECISIONS 2026-10-25 "Stone is mined: blows that run by themselves, as felling, training digging"). Tiers 1 to 3 are open (`open_tiers` 3, content/core/params.json:88); metal is refused by the check (tools/src/check.ts:356, :362) until its discovery (G45; DECISIONS 2026-10-09 "Metal: the grammar is ready, the content waits for its discovery"); nothing of a fight is built (G35, docs/design/GAME_RULES.md:400). Making has no level wall (G40; kernel/src/step.ts:223-235), gathering has one (kernel/src/step.ts:619). Clothing never wears (docs/design/PROGRESSION.md:471). The trader buys at the item's value from the value table and mints the gold (kernel/src/step.ts:972-986; `salePrice`, :997-1009); the free market, a stock per item in each world, is decided and not built (DECISIONS 2026-10-09 "The free market comes right after crafting wave 4, one per world, and the demo tunes it", DECISIONS.md:646): no market or stock code exists in kernel/src.
