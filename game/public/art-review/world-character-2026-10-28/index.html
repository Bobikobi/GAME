Forager — human A

The connected world material study reuses the picked human A character, the male Forager, rendered to pixel sprites at a 50-degree camera elevation.

Attribution: Forager, generated with Meshy (meshy.ai), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).

The original model is game/public3d/models/character-forager-m.glb. Its recorded modifications are: scaled to 1.64 m; skeleton and skin weights added in Blender by fitting the warrior's Mixamo-named rig to the body, with the belt end cut free where it touched the left hand; Idle, Walk and Run clips retargeted from the warrior; two empty prop sockets added; reduced from 84,584 to 50,000 triangles and texture maps reduced from 2048 to 1024 pixels.

The sprite derivative is posed with library motion, rendered at 50 degrees and snapped to the game's palette by tools/anim. Motion uses Quaternius Universal Animation Library, CC0 1.0, action Jog_Fwd_Loop, retargeted to the Forager's Mixamo rig.

These three sheets are copied without pixel changes from repository GAME, branch feat/races-in-game, commit d6e324803: content/kit/core/chars/human_down.png, human_up.png and human_left.png. The right-facing view mirrors the left sheet.

Each frame is 128 × 128 art pixels, with its ground anchor at [64,90]. Ten walking poses are followed by a standing pose. The study displays the sprite at native scale 1 and selects walking poses from actual distance travelled. The source records are tools/anim/anim.json:targets.forager, tools/anim/clips/jog_human.json, content/kit/core/kit.json and game/public3d/models/README.md.

The owner's 27 October 2026 human A selection is recorded in the source branch's DECISIONS.md. The study's copies and SHA-256 hashes are documented in docs/design/frames/rich-world-character-2026-10-28/provenance.json.
