Forager — human A

The connected world material study reuses the picked human A character, the male Forager, rendered to pixel sprites at a 50-degree camera elevation.

Attribution: Forager, generated with Meshy (meshy.ai), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).

The original model is game/public3d/models/character-forager-m.glb. Its recorded modifications are: scaled to 1.64 m; skeleton and skin weights added in Blender by fitting the warrior's Mixamo-named rig to the body, with the belt end cut free where it touched the left hand; Idle, Walk and Run clips retargeted from the warrior; two empty prop sockets added; reduced from 84,584 to 50,000 triangles and texture maps reduced from 2048 to 1024 pixels.

The sprite derivative is posed with library motion, rendered at 50 degrees and snapped to the game's palette by tools/anim. Motion uses Quaternius Universal Animation Library, CC0 1.0, action Jog_Fwd_Loop, retargeted to the Forager's Mixamo rig.

On 29 October 2026 the same model and library action were rendered again with the retained renderer from feat/races-in-game, commit d6e324803. Five views include projected diagonals; right-facing views mirror the corresponding left views. The previous three copied sheets remain preserved as historical comparison material.

Each frame is 128 × 128 art pixels, with its ground anchor at [64,90]. Thirty walking poses are followed by a standing pose. Native scale 1 is retained. Actual travelled distance advances one continuous cycle phase, including when direction changes. At the study's 125 art px/s speed, playback remains under 30 poses/s.

The owner's human A selection remains the appearance reference. The new render's source hashes, frame geometry, projected feet and per-view cycle distances are in docs/design/frames/rich-world-motion-2026-10-29/walk-metadata.json. The source action is still Jog_Fwd_Loop; the study's denser strips do not replace production race sheets or change their import limits.
