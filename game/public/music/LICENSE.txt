The music by state: routine.ogg, night.ogg, hunting.ogg, combat.ogg, near-death.ogg

The tunes and their scores are the game's own (game/src/audio/pieces.ts), released under the
repository's code licence (AGPL-3.0-only).

They are played on sampled instruments from the FluidR3_GM soundfont by Frank Wen, as
prerendered by gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts (https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts),
released under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 licence:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Instruments used: recorder, flute, French horn, nylon guitar, pizzicato strings, orchestral
harp, string ensemble 1, acoustic bass, celesta, taiko drum, woodblock.

Rendered on 30.10 (docs/design/SOUND.md section 5): each piece laid twice end to end in an
OfflineAudioContext at 44.1 kHz with the second pass kept, so the loop has no seam, then
encoded as Ogg Vorbis (the routine at 80 kbps, the loops at 96 kbps).
