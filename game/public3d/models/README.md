## Props (`prop-*`)

`prop-spade.glb`, `prop-basket.glb` and `prop-hat.glb` are not from Kenney either: they were made
for this repository in Blender by `game/tools/blender/props.py`, which draws their shapes and every
colour and normal map in code, so they hold no outside asset and need no attribution. Each names in
its node extras the socket it hangs from on a person rigged by `game/tools/blender/person_rig.py`
(`socket_back` for the spade and the basket, `socket_head` for the hat), and each was fitted to the
people who carry it: the spade to `character-digger-m.glb` and `character-digger-f.glb`, the
basket and its straps to `character-forager-m.glb` and `character-forager-f.glb`, and the hat to
`character-explorer-m.glb` and `character-explorer-f.glb`. What lies on the body, the straps and the
hat, is fitted to each person in turn, and the file holds one copy per person, named by the look in
its node extras (`wearer`). The cell trial puts the props on by role (`?look=digger-f`,
`?look=forager-m`, `?look=explorer-f`) or by `?props=`, with the copy of that look (the first copy on
any other).
